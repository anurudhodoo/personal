Chapter One Office

The day I saw you first

We were in the same office, but life kept us in different corners: different teams, different reasons to be busy. Then one day I saw you at the office cafe, and the whole place felt like it had been waiting for that exact second. You looked effortless, quiet, beautiful in a way that did not need attention to still completely take mine.

I did not know your laugh properly yet. I did not know your moods, your shy smile, your little expressions, or how much peace you would bring me. But something in me recognized you before I had the words for it. After that day, you were not just someone from office. You were the person my eyes searched for without asking me first.

Chapter Two Cafe, Unplanned

The conversation that stayed

It was supposed to be a small talk. A few minutes, maybe. But somehow we stayed. The cafe noise faded, the time passed too quickly, and I started noticing the details that would later become my favorite things about you: the softness in your voice, the way you listened, the cute seriousness on your face, and that small smile that made me forget what I was going to say next.

And yes, your smell. I still do not know how to say it without sounding completely gone, but it pulled me closer like a secret only I had noticed. That day, I walked away pretending to be normal, but inside I knew I was already in trouble. The sweetest kind.

Chapter Three The Everyday Ride

The slow way became my favorite

You were a day scholar, going home the same way every evening. I could have treated that as a simple coincidence, but I wanted more minutes with you. So I chose the slow way on purpose. I chose the route where I could sit near you, talk to you, watch you become less shy, and carry one more piece of you with me when the day ended.

Those rides were never just rides for me. They were tiny promises before we knew we were making them. Then one evening my hand found yours. No announcement, no perfect timing, no rehearsed courage. Just two hands that had been waiting, finally brave enough to stay together.

Chapter Four Bun Club

The first kiss changed everything

Bun Club will always mean something different to me. It is not just a place anymore. It is the place where all the almost moments, all the bus rides, all the shy looks, and all the feelings we were trying to hide finally reached one beautiful point.

You looked so pretty that day it felt unfair. A little nervous, a little soft, completely you. Then the kiss happened, and the world became very quiet for a second. After that, there was no way to go back to being just colleagues, just friends, or just two people who happened to meet. You became my favorite truth.

Chapter Five My Babee

You became home in a person

Somewhere between office corridors, cafe tables, bus seats, you stopped being just someone I liked. You became my babee. My soft place. My chaos and calm. My favorite notification. The person whose presence can fix a bad day without even trying.

We did not need a perfect proposal to become real. Before last August, we simply started trying, and that quiet beginning became the best decision of my life. One year later, I still choose your laugh, your stubbornness, your sweetness, your angry face, your shy smile, your hand in mine, and every ordinary day that has you inside it.
