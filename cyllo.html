01 / Foundation

Overview & Vision

Cyllo is an open-source ERP and CRM platform bringing essential business operations—accounting, human resources, inventory management, supply chain, and customer relationships—into one connected digital ecosystem.

Unlike traditional legacy enterprise solutions that suffer from clunky navigation and steep onboarding curves, Cyllo was conceived as an agile, AI-augmented, modern platform that empowers businesses of all sizes to automate operations with clarity.

“Enterprise software shouldn’t require a 300-page manual to understand. Good design makes powerful tools feel obvious.”

02 / Execution

Design Scope

I worked on defining the end-to-end design language and interface architecture for both the Cyllo platform product and its digital presence:

ERP Platform Architecture

Designed modular dashboard workspaces, data tables with inline editing, batch actions, and advanced relational filters.

AI & Predictive Tools

Crafted conversational and contextual AI interfaces for sales forecasting, cash flow trends, and inventory anomaly detection.

Public Web Experience

Designed the landing pages, interactive product showcases, pricing tiers, and developer documentation layout at cyllo.com.

Component System

Established a design system with scalable tokens, high-density table states, data visualization palettes, and dark mode support.

03 / Ecosystem

Connected Workflows

The platform unites multiple mission-critical modules under a unified interaction model:

01 Finance

02 Commercial

03 Logistics

Employee directory, automated payroll calculations, attendance tracking, shift scheduling, and self-service requests.

04 / Learnings

Key UX Challenges

01

Balancing Enterprise Power with Visual Lightness

Enterprise users need deep data tables, but cluttered interfaces induce mental fatigue. Solution: Progressive data disclosure, customizable column views, and generous typography hierarchy.

02

Seamless Navigation Across Modules

Prevented siloed experiences by integrating a universal Command K launcher, breadcrumbs, and cross-module deep linking.

03

Clear Brand Narrative for an Open-Source Product

Bridging the gap between corporate enterprise buyers and open-source developers through clear value propositions and friction-free sandbox trials.

05 / Impact

Outcome & Reflection

The Cyllo project demonstrated that open-source enterprise software can look as polished and intuitive as leading consumer SaaS tools.

By harmonizing product functionality with an engaging web presence, Cyllo successfully positioned itself as a modern, forward-thinking alternative to bloated legacy systems.
