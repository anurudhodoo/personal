01 / Context

The Vision

Horilla is a modern open-source Human Resource Management software designed to automate people operations. From hiring and onboarding to daily attendance, shift planning, appraisals, and payroll, Horilla serves both enterprise HR managers and everyday employees.

My objective was to eliminate the clunky friction typical of enterprise HR tools, turning everyday administrative tasks into quick, pleasant interactions.

“Employees interact with HR software when they need something important: booking time off, checking their paycheck, or clocking in. Any confusion causes frustration.”

03 / Strategy

UX Decisions

01

Two Distinct User Mindsets

Designed a split UX architecture: a data-dense, keyboard-friendly command view for HR administrators, and a clean, high-clarity view for employees.

02

Visualizing Complex Leave Policies

Replaced dense text policies with visual donut meters showing earned, used, and remaining leave days at a single glance.

03

Zero-Latency Approval Tray

Managers can review and approve leave or expense requests directly from notification toasts without leaving their active dashboard.

04 / Impact

Outcome & Results

Horilla achieved rapid community adoption thanks to its intuitive user experience, saving HR administrators countless hours each week in paperwork and reducing employee support tickets.

The system was recognized for having one of the cleanest open-source HR dashboards in the modern web landscape.
