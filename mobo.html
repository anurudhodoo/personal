01 / Foundation

Overview & Context

Mobo is a family of mobile applications built for Odoo users, bringing complex business workflows from Odoo into focused, mobile-friendly experiences across Sales, CRM, Delivery, Inventory, Manufacturing, Fleet, Attendance, and POS.

The challenge was not simply to make Odoo responsive on mobile. It was to rethink how information-heavy enterprise workflows could become clear, usable, and efficient on a much smaller screen, while maintaining consistency across an expanding ecosystem of applications.

“Odoo contains a vast amount of business information. On desktop, users have space to view tables, fields, actions, and sidebars together. Mobile is fundamentally different.”

02 / Execution

My Role & Scope

As the UI/UX Designer at Cybrosys Technologies, I led the user experience and interface design across the Mobo product ecosystem from foundation to launch:

Workflow & Information Architecture

Studied complex business processes with Odoo developers to identify usability bottlenecks and streamline user journeys.

Mobo Design System

Established the scalable visual identity, typography, tokens, interaction states, and reusable Flutter UI component patterns.

Mobile Adaptation

Rethought desktop-oriented enterprise layouts (multi-column tables, relational lookups) into progressive handheld patterns.

Developer Collaboration

Partnered side-by-side with Flutter engineers during sprint builds, reviewing live builds and refining edge-case interactions.

03 / Core Challenge

The Sales Order Dilemma

The Sales Order screen presented one of the most difficult UX problems. The desktop Odoo workflow contained a dense, table-like layout with 16+ columns: line items, unit prices, tax formulas, discount percentages, stock availability, and delivery statuses.

Simply shrinking the table made content illegible. Stripping data would cripple sales reps who depend on instant pricing and availability in front of clients.

Desktop Odoo Structure

Horizontal Table Clutter

Designed for large monitors with mouse precision and unlimited width.

  • ✕ 16 dense columns requiring constant horizontal scrolling
  • ✕ Tiny action buttons buried inside cell dropdowns
  • ✕ Context loss when verifying totals and line discounts
Mobo Mobile Architecture

Progressive Disclosure Cards

Re-architected around thumb reach zones and clear scanning hierarchy.

  • ✓ Card-based hierarchy with primary total & status front and center
  • ✓ Expandable bottom sheets for tax, lines, and customer details
  • ✓ Sticky bottom primary bar for one-tap Quotation / Order confirmation
Desktop Structure → Data Analysis → Mobile Wireframes → Alternative Layouts → Final Flutter UI
04 / Foundation

Design System & Viva Magenta

Mobo was built to scale across dozens of Odoo modules. Designing each application independently would inevitably produce inconsistencies. I established a shared, tokenized design system to create harmony across teams.

I proposed Viva Magenta as the primary visual direction, inspired by Pantone’s Color of the Year. This gave Mobo an energetic, modern, premium signature that breaks away from drab, sterile enterprise software.

05 / Scalability

Designing for Multiple Modules

A design system does not mean every screen should look identical. While the underlying components remained consistent, the UX adapted to the unique operational demands of each workflow:

Sales ≠ CRM ≠ Delivery ≠ Inventory ≠ Attendance

01 Commercial

Mobo Sales

Quotations, order conversions, customer credit status, and instant on-site invoice generation with digital customer signature.

02 Pipelines

Mobo CRM

Visual stage pipelines, meeting schedules, lead scores, quick phone call logging, and automated WhatsApp/email touchpoints.

03 Logistics

Mobo Delivery

Turn-by-turn route dispatching, proof-of-delivery photo capture, real-time customer status updates, and signature collection.

04 Warehouse

Mobo Inventory

High-speed camera barcode scanning, internal bin transfers, stock audit counts, and real-time multi-location sync.

05 Workforce

Mobo Attendance

Geofenced mobile punch-in/out, biometric verification, overtime requests, and instant leave approval workflows.

06 Operations

Mobo POS & Fleet

Offline-first cashier register, vehicle fuel logging, maintenance alerts, and odometer tracking on the move.

07 / Synthesis

Challenges & Takeaways

01

High Information Density

Odoo workflows pack dense data points. Solution: established clear progressive hierarchy instead of blindly deleting critical fields.

02

Desktop-to-Mobile Adaptation

Tables cannot merely be made responsive. We redesigned interactions around single-thumb reach zones, bottom drawers, and sticky action triggers.

03

Module Diversity

Each module behaves differently. The solution was a shared foundation + module-specific UX that feels coherent yet purpose-built.

04

Maintaining Consistency at Scale

Created tokenized Figma libraries and code-level specs so new modules can be launched without reinventing the design wheel.

05

Design-Development Sync

Direct daily communication with Flutter devs ensured rapid problem solving when complex Odoo nested objects were encountered.

08 / Reflection

Designing for Complexity

The biggest takeaway from Mobo was simple: Complexity isn’t always something you can remove. Sometimes you have to design a better way to navigate it.

Working on Mobo taught me to look beyond individual screens and think about systems, workflows, information hierarchy, and scalability. It proved that great enterprise UX happens right in the sweet spot between user empathy, business workflows, and technical feasibility.
