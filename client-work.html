01 / Perspective

Direct Delivery

Working directly with international stakeholders requires translating complex business goals, localization needs, and domain-specific requirements into clean digital experiences.

These engagements spanned consumer eCommerce, B2B wholesale trade platforms, and enterprise field logistics, demanding adaptability across different user personas and constraints.

“Client delivery is where product strategy meets real business deadlines. Every design decision must directly serve user clarity and operational efficiency.”

02 / Projects

Featured Deliveries

01 Ireland · eCommerce

Clippers Ireland

Redesigned the direct-to-consumer and professional salon equipment storefront with fast faceted search, visual product comparisons, and a streamlined 3-step checkout.

02 Global · B2B Wholesale

Oha Trade

Architected a high-volume B2B portal with dynamic tiered pricing, multi-currency invoices, credit term management, and automated reorder schedules.

03 Enterprise · Logistics

Averigo

Designed fleet management and field service tracking tools featuring live route mapping, driver dispatch schedules, and proof-of-delivery signatures.

03 / Learnings

What Direct Delivery Taught Me

01

Translating Ambiguous Requirements

Stakeholders often describe problems from an operational perspective. My role was to unpack those requirements, identify the root UX bottlenecks, and present clear solutions.

02

Localization & Multi-Currency Clarity

Designed flexible currency formatters, localized address fields, and tax displays that effortlessly adjust across European and North American markets.

03

Cross-Discipline Alignment

Maintained continuous synchronization between overseas project managers, developers, and QA engineers to ensure flawless fidelity from Figma to production.
